Perceive. Plan.
Dissect.

I contributed to autonomous-dissection systems that coordinate tissue grasping and cautery along a visual boundary. The demonstrations connect image-based planning with closed-loop robot motion in deformable environments.

Multimodal surgical data · ISMR / JMRR

CRCD

I co-created the Comprehensive Robotic Cholecystectomy Dataset, connecting endoscopic video with robot kinematics, pedal signals, and surgical annotations. It provides synchronized observations for studying surgical perception and robot behavior.
